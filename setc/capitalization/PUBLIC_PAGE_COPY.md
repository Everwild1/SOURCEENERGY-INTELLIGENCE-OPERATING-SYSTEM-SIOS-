## Recommended title while the public live-claim gate is disabled

# Interbank Network Registry & Connectivity Status

## Subtitle

SourceEnergy Capitalization Block institutional registry, relationship status, and governed connectivity classifications.

## Executive summary

The SourceEnergy Interbank Network Registry organizes financial institutions, treasury entities, infrastructure providers, and sovereign nodes that are relevant to the Capitalization Block architecture. Each record is assigned SourceEnergy internal identifiers and a governed status covering institutional relationship, technical connectivity, and verification.

A registry entry does not by itself establish that the named institution is a SourceEnergy partner, participant, custodian, account provider, correspondent bank, settlement provider, or live technical connection. Those claims may be displayed only after the required legal, compliance, technical, and governance evidence has been independently verified.

## Required disclosure banner

> **Registry disclosure:** Dominion Cube IDs and Relay Codes are SourceEnergy internal identifiers. Unless an entry is explicitly labeled **Verified live connection**, no contractual banking relationship, production integration, account access, custody authority, regulatory approval, or settlement connectivity is asserted.

## Dashboard metrics

Display only:

- published registry entries
- evidence-backed relationships
- verified live connections
- public live-claim gate status
- last projection refresh timestamp

Do not display treasury balances, capital commitments, settlement amounts, reserve values, or exposures on the public page.

## Status legend

**Registry target** — The institution or node is included for architecture, research, outreach, due diligence, or strategic planning. No verified institutional relationship is asserted.

**Evidence-backed relationship; production status not publicly asserted** — Internal evidence supports a contractual or integration-stage relationship, but the public system is not asserting a verified production connection.

**Verified live connection** — Legal/relationship evidence, institution verification, technical production evidence, verification timestamps, and the public governance gate have all been satisfied.

**Not connected** — No approved technical connection is recorded.

**Sandbox/Test/Certification** — A non-production technical stage. These classifications must never be described as live production connectivity.

## Footer statement

The dashboard is a SourceEnergy governance and observability interface. External institutions, regulated rails, custodians, and authoritative ledgers retain their own legal, regulatory, operational, and settlement authority.

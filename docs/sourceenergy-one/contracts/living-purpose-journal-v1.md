# SourceEnergy One — Living Purpose Journal Contract v1

Status: Draft governed contract

## Purpose

SourceEnergy One treats purpose as capable of refinement through lived experience without allowing private narrative to silently rewrite an authoritative Genesis record.

Canonical lifecycle:

Purpose Discovery → Codex24 → 4P Synthesis → Human Confirmation → Genesis G1 → Living Purpose Journal → Experience/Reflection Intelligence → proposed 4P evolution → Human Confirmation → superseding Genesis G2 when materially warranted.

## Separation of records

### Genesis
Genesis is an immutable, human-approved checkpoint. It contains approved summaries, provenance, evidence references, hashes and the governed 4P baseline. Existing Genesis payloads are never edited to reflect later experience.

### Living Purpose Journal
The journal is a private, longitudinal source record owned by the subject. Entries may contain lived experience, reflection, learning, relationships, achievements, setbacks, priorities, opportunities and observations relevant to Purpose, Product, People and Profit.

Raw journal narrative is protected source material by default. It is not automatically included in Genesis, SETC, SourceCube, institutional records or execution contexts.

### Reflection synthesis
SourceEnergy One/Codex24 may derive structured purpose signals and proposed 4P changes from journal entries. Derived artifacts must retain provenance to the source entries while respecting consent and disclosure boundaries.

AI synthesis is counsel, not authority. A proposed purpose or 4P change does not modify Genesis.

## Human confirmation gate

Material 4P evolution requires explicit human review and confirmation before it may become an authoritative lifecycle event. Confirmation must identify the proposed changes, evidence/provenance, authorizing actor, consent scope and attestation.

## Genesis supersession

When confirmed evolution is material enough to alter the authoritative 4P baseline, SourceEnergy One creates a new Genesis package referencing `prior_genesis_id`. The prior Genesis remains immutable and historically valid for its effective period.

G1 → experience history → confirmed evolution → G2 → experience history → confirmed evolution → G3

Supersession is append-only. It is not mutation.

## 4P journal semantics

- Purpose: meaning, direction, values, calling, intended impact.
- Product: capabilities, artifacts, services, ventures and expressions through which purpose becomes useful.
- People: collaborators, communities, beneficiaries, institutions, relationships and governance participants.
- Profit: sustainable value creation, resource regeneration and economic viability. Journal or synthesis content concerning Profit never constitutes transaction authority, investment advice, promised returns or permission to move capital.

## Privacy and consent

1. Raw narrative is private by default.
2. Journal access and derived use require explicit scope.
3. SETC receives governed 4P state/evidence references, not unrestricted raw narrative.
4. SourceCube receives only information authorized for its orchestration purpose.
5. Consequential actions remain subject to policy, authorization and execution controls independent of journal content.
6. Revocation of future processing does not rewrite already-authorized immutable historical records; subsequent use must respect the current consent state and applicable governance.

## Minimum journal entry contract

A journal entry should include:
- journal_entry_id
- subject_id
- recorded_at
- effective_at (optional)
- narrative or protected content reference
- content_hash
- consent_scope_id
- visibility classification
- source type
- prior_entry_id (optional)
- created_by / actor reference
- status

## Minimum derived reflection contract

A reflection synthesis should include:
- reflection_id
- subject_id
- source_entry_ids
- source_hashes
- synthesis version/model provenance
- Purpose/Product/People/Profit signals
- materiality assessment
- proposed changes (if any)
- confidence/evidence characterization
- consent scope
- human review status

## Governance invariants

- Journal content cannot directly execute a consequential action.
- AI cannot approve its own proposed 4P evolution.
- Raw journal narrative cannot silently enter Genesis.
- Raw journal narrative cannot silently enter SETC or SourceCube.
- Genesis cannot be edited by journal activity.
- A superseding Genesis requires the same governed human-confirmation boundary as initial Genesis creation.
- The system must preserve provenance from a confirmed change back to the evidence used to propose it.

## Architectural responsibility

SourceEnergy One owns journaling UX, privacy, reflection and human confirmation. Codex24 provides synthesis/counsel. Genesis provides immutable approved checkpoints. SETC natively understands the approved 4P economic ontology. SourceCube interprets the current authorized state for orchestration. SIOS remains the controlled integration/execution path.

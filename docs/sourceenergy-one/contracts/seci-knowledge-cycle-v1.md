# SourceEnergy One — SECI Knowledge Cycle Contract v1

Status: Draft governed contract

SourceEnergy One models SECI as a first-class knowledge-expansion spiral adjacent to, but distinct from, the Living Purpose Journal and the 4P economic ontology.

## Canonical spiral

Socialize → Externalize → Combine → Internalize → Socialize …

The spiral may repeat indefinitely. A SECI cycle does not by itself modify Genesis or authorize consequential action.

## Semantics

- Socialize: tacit knowledge develops through relationships, observation, participation, mentoring, collaboration and lived experience.
- Externalize: tacit knowledge becomes explicit through journal entries, ideas, narratives, diagrams, hypotheses, lessons and other artifacts.
- Combine: authorized explicit knowledge is synthesized with other governed knowledge, research, prior artifacts and Codex24 analysis.
- Internalize: the person learns, practices, experiments, builds or changes behavior; resulting experience becomes evidence for subsequent cycles.

## Relationship to 4P

SECI expands knowledge. 4P interprets the purpose/economic significance of that knowledge. SECI artifacts and insights may be associated with Purpose, Product, People and/or Profit, but do not alter the authoritative 4P baseline without human confirmation and Genesis governance.

## Relationship to Living Purpose Journal

The Journal is an important Externalize mechanism but is not synonymous with SECI. Journal narrative remains private by default. Knowledge artifacts inherit explicit consent and visibility boundaries.

## Relationship to Genesis

Many SECI cycles may occur between Genesis states. Material knowledge evolution may contribute evidence to a reflection and 4P evolution candidate. The governed chain is:

SECI cycles → evidence → Codex24/reflection → proposed 4P evolution → human confirmation → impact assessment → Genesis approval → superseding Genesis.

Genesis remains immutable and append-only.

## Persistence model

- `knowledge_cycles`: subject, context, consent, current SECI phase, lifecycle and prior-cycle linkage.
- `knowledge_artifacts`: protected phase-specific artifacts with database-computed content hashes, provenance and visibility.
- `knowledge_transitions`: evidence-backed phase transitions.
- `knowledge_insights`: Codex24/AI-derived counsel linked to source hashes and 4P dimensions.

## Governance invariants

1. Phase progression is ordered: Socialize → Externalize → Combine → Internalize → Socialize.
2. A transition requires evidence from the current phase.
3. Raw/private artifacts are not automatically disclosed to SETC, SourceCube or Genesis.
4. AI-derived knowledge insight has `counsel` authority by default.
5. AI cannot confirm its own insight or change the authoritative 4P state.
6. SECI knowledge cannot directly authorize execution, transactions or institutional commitments.
7. Consent scope and provenance must survive derivation.
8. Genesis supersession remains a separate human-governed lifecycle boundary.

## SourceEnergy One responsibility

SourceEnergy One owns the participant experience, consent, artifact capture, knowledge-cycle state, Codex24 counsel and human review. SETC may consume governed knowledge state through explicit contracts. SourceCube may use authorized derived state for contextual intelligence and orchestration. SIOS remains the controlled execution/integration path.

## Constitutional sequence

SOURCE -> Fear -> Presence -> Wisdom -> Knowledge -> Understanding -> Counsel -> Might/Power -> Love invariant -> 4P -> Authority -> Manifestation -> Experience -> SECI -> ReGenesis

The canonical seven Genesis dimensions are, in order:

1. Fear of the Lord
2. Presence of the Lord
3. Wisdom
4. Knowledge
5. Understanding
6. Counsel
7. Might / Power

Love is not an eighth dimension. The constitutional rule is:

`Selfless = Love`

Love is represented as a separate completion/invariance assessment that follows successful 7D traversal.

## Machine-enforced readiness

A governed decision is authorization-ready only when all of the following are satisfied:

- all seven 7D evaluations are complete with no FAIL state;
- the Love invariant evaluation is PASS and human-reviewed;
- all four 4P evaluations are complete;
- all four SECI evaluations are complete;
- the scientific firewall passes;
- required evidence and approval references exist.

Conceptually:

`7D AND Love AND 4P AND SECI AND ScientificFirewall AND NoFailure => AuthorizationReady`

## Authority boundary

Capability does not imply authority. Might/Power supplies bounded execution capacity; it does not override the Love invariant, human accountability, evidence requirements, or execution authorization.
